# Affect and Embodiment 

This section includes papers that develop theory and report on research and experimentation with affect and embodiment. While I have been interested in affect and emotion since the beginning of my academic career, a more direct turn toward affect and embodiment has been part of my work for over ten years. Much of this work has been inspired by Deleuzian theory.
